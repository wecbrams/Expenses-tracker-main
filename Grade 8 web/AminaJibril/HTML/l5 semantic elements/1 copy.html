Facts about Dogs

Dogs have a sense of time but do not understand it the same way humans do. They cannot measure minutes or hours, but they can remember daily routines and anticipate events, such as when it is time for a walk or meal.

2. Their History

Dogs are descendants of ancient wolves. They were domesticated by humans more than 15,000 years ago, even before farming began. They were among the first animals to live and work alongside people.

Did You Know?

Dogs can be trained to understand commands, detect emotions, and even help humans with important tasks such as guiding the blind and detecting illnesses.
