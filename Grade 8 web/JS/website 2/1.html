Pizza

Pizza is a savory dish of Italian origin consisting of a usually round, flattened base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and various other ingredients. A person who makes pizza is known as a pizzaiolo.

Pizza

Pizza is baked at a high temperature, traditionally in a wood-fired oven. A small pizza is sometimes called a pizzetta and it is enjoyed all over the world.
